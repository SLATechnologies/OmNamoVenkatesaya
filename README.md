# Om Namo Venkatesaya Android Application Privacy Policy

**App:** Om Namo Venkatesaya\
**Developer:** SLA Technologies\
**Last updated:** 13 November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sri Venkateswara Swamy – Om Namo Venkatesaya.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application operates and how user information is handled.

Sri Venkateswara Swamy is a devotional application designed to provide users with a spiritual experience through divine darshan, traditional pooja activities, devotional offerings, temple animations and devotional audio.

The application is designed to operate offline and does not collect or transmit users' personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, request, access or store personally identifiable information from users.

Specifically, the application does not collect:

- Name or personal identification details.
- Email addresses or telephone numbers.
- Physical location or GPS information.
- Contacts or address book information.
- Photos, videos or personal files.
- Financial or payment information.
- Browsing history or personal usage profiles.

No user registration or account creation is required for the app's devotional functionality.

3. Offline Functionality

Sri Venkateswara Swamy is designed to function offline.

The devotional features, including:

- Lord Venkateswara Swamy Darshan.
- Seven Doors Darshan experience.
- Deepam and Pushpam offerings.
- Prasadam and traditional pooja activities.
- Temple bell and Harathi.
- Namaskaram.
- Available devotional audio and animations.

are intended to operate without requiring an internet connection.

4. Third-Party Services

The application does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms, tracking services or external data collection services.

The application does not share personal information with third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

The application does not maintain a remote server or cloud-based database for collecting users' personal information.

No personal information is transmitted to external servers by the application.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is a devotional application intended for users interested in Hindu spirituality and devotional activities.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

7. Internet and Device Permissions

The application is designed for offline devotional use. Any device permissions, if requested by a particular version, will be used only for the corresponding application functionality.

The application does not use device permissions to collect personal information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect application improvements, functionality changes or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated Privacy Policy will be published at the same official location.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Since the application does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal information held by us for users to request access to, correction of or deletion from our servers.
